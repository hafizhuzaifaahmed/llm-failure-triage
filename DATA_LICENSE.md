# Data licence (proposed; the authors confirm before release)

- **Code** (`src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `paper/` sources): MIT, see `LICENSE`.
- **Data produced by this study** (labels, evidence packages, model decisions and call logs, analysis outputs under
  `results/`, `data/processed/`, `data/ground_truth_store/`, `data/raw/llm_calls_local*`): proposed **CC BY 4.0**.
- **Third-party material is not relicensed.** Generated tests, focal-method code, Javadoc and fix diffs are derived
  from the Defects4J 3.0.1 subject projects (each under its own open-source licence, mostly Apache-2.0) and from the
  replication package of Zhao et al.; Defects4J itself is MIT. Model outputs are subject to the licences of the models
  that produced them (Qwen3.5, Gemma 4); the model weights are **not** redistributed.
